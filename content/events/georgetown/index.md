  A deep dive into the hidden environmental and systemic costs of the AI revolution. 
  We explore why AI demand is projected to double by 2030 and what that means for global energy grids, water

abstract: >-
  While AI promises transformative potential for energy systems, its current physical footprint is 
  staggering. An AI-generated query consumes ten times more energy than a 
  standard search, and training a single large-scale model can produce emissions equivalent to five times 
  the lifetime of an average passenger car.
  
  This talk examines the "Reality of AI's Lifecycle," from the 300,000 gallons of water consumed daily by 
  mid-sized data centers to the regulatory backlash leading to "kill switches" on energy grids.
  
  We discuss the transition from traditional Machine Learning to Generative AI in high-stakes sectors like 
  finance and evaluate whether industry mitigation efforts, like liquid cooling and smaller, efficient 
  models, are enough to offset this growing footprint.
